=========================
APP PLANNING & DESIGN
=========================

1. PROJECT IDEA
-------------------------
- What is your app called?
Tenna-Themed Journal App
- What type of app is it? (journal, listings, notes, etc.) 
    Profile and Notes
- What will users use your app for?
    They will use the app to write down notes with titles or dates.

2. DATA MODEL (WHAT ARE YOU STORING?)
-------------------------
- What is a single “entry” in your app?
    It's a Title and the content that the user entered with it.
- What fields will each entry have? (title, content, date, etc.)
    Title, content. (user can put date in the title)
- How will entries be connected to a user?
    When each user logs in the dashboard will welcome them specifically, and the 
    dashboard will only show the entries that user put in.
3. CORE FEATURES (CRUD)
-------------------------
- What can users CREATE?
    Users can create a note "pad" to write it. 
- What will they see on the main page (READ)?
    They will see a welcome page with their name and the date. 
- What can users EDIT (UPDATE)?
    Their note entries, they can edit what they say.
- What can users DELETE?
    Again, their note entries.
4. USER FLOW
-------------------------
- What happens after a user logs in?
    They are brought to their dashboard that shows them all their entries, an add
    entry button, and a logout button.
- What pages/routes will your app need? (dashboard, create, edit, etc.)
    Dashboard, create, edit, delete, sign-up, log-in, log-out.
- Describe a typical user experience from login → using your app
    Login-> see dashboard->Click add entry->Write what you want in the entry boxes-> Click submit
    ->view entries in dashboard->delete or edit them if you want-> sign out
5. USER EXPERIENCE (UX)
-------------------------
- How should your app look and feel?
    We are keeping it simple, but most of the team decided on a specific color-scheme.
- How will users navigate between pages?
    Buttons
- How will entries be displayed (list, cards, etc.)?
    A list on a seperate card than the welcome, create, and logout button.
6. OPTIONAL FEATURES
-------------------------
- What extra features might you add? (search, tags, sorting, etc.)
    Won't have time for any.
